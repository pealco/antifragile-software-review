Use when the user wants to find, rank, or reduce what would hurt a software system under stress, even if they never say "antifragile": irreversible or hard-to-undo operations (data migrations, bulk updates, deploys), single points of failure, missing rollback or feedback loops, outages or incidents that never become tests or alerts, dependency and vendor lock-in decisions, or applying Taleb's antifragility ideas to a codebase or architecture. Reviews the repository, traces one critical flow, scores exposure, then either reports findings (audit wording: review, assess) or patches the highest-risk reversible issue (action wording: harden, fix, make). Takes a repo path or focus. Not for line-level bug review, security audits, performance tuning, or narrow feature work.
